A single player card game version of [[Scoundrel]].
# Rules
- Start with 20 health
- Six sided dice
- You can't skip two rooms in a row
- Barehand damage is dependent on dice roll
### Monsters
- Swords and wands are monsters. The number on them is the # of damage they deal to you
	- Knight: 11 damage
	- Queen: 12 damage
	- King: 13 damage
	- Ace: 14 damage
- Pages are monster scouts, they find the first monster from discard pile and bring it to the room. You must deal with the monster scout before dealing with all other cards.
	- If there are no monsters in discard pile, then page is unsuccessful and dies
### Healing
- Cups 2-10 are healing potions equal to the value on the card. You can hold one for later use or keep an extra one with the maiden
- Ace of cups heals in full
### Weapons
- Pentacles 2-10 are weapons
- You can carry one weapon at a time unless you have maiden which allows two
- Weapons negate damage by an enemy by the count on the weapon
- Weapons can only be used against enemies of lower # than the previous enemy killed
- if no enemy was killed, weapons can be used on any enemy
- You can choose to attack an enemy barehanded to preserve the weapon
- Ace of pentacles is a throwing dagger. It adds 5 damage to your attack. Discard after you attack.
### Allies
- Only for cups and pentacles
- Page is a squire. If there is a monster you can use the page to distract and remove the monster.
- Knight is a blacksmith. Blacksmith can repair weapon by removing all monsters on the weapon
	- If the weapon is undamaged, you can upgrade it. Put the knight under the weapon and now the weapon is +1 damage
- Queens are maidens. They store healing or weapons. The moment you take what you stored, you must discard her
- Kings are heros. they add an extra dice roll for you to add to your barehand damage. You discard the hero after you use the dice roll
### Major Arcana
- Wheel of fortune shuffles draw pile
- Magician, High preistess, empress, emperor, lovers, chariot, temperance, star, sun, world are boons
	- Heal 6 HP or gain a weapons of 8 strength
- Heirophant, Strength, Hermit, Justice, Hanged one, Death, Devil, Tower, Moon, Judgement are banes
	- Lost 6HP or discard current weapon
# Game Start
- Remove the fool from the deck, this is you
- Shuffle deck
- Setup a discard pile empty
- Setup draw pile with all cards
- Take and lay out four cards from draw pile
# Round
- If you skip a room, scoop all cards in a motion and put them at the bottom of the draw pile
- You can resolve 3/4 cards per room
# End
- Game ends when you fail to fill a room with 4 cards
