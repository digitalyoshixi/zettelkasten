Parameters used to modulate sound.
They are how [[Synthesizer]] generate music.
![[Attack Decay Sustain Release-20260910030554742.webp]]
- Attack: how long it takes for sound to reach full volume
- Delay: How long it takes for sound to go to sustain level
- Sustain: Level of sound that maintains when you hold a note
- Release: How long it takes for sound to return to silence
