The belief that the [[Immortal Soul]]'s memories about [[Platonic Form|Ideal forms]] can be recollected while one is alive.
# Argument
```
P1: Ideal forms and objects that possess the forms are not the same
P2: Knowledge about ideal forms cannot be gained by studying imperfect forms found in the physical world
P3: Knowledge of ideal forms can only be obtained when the immortal soul is seperated from the body (so senses dont inhibit reason)
P4: Humans have the ability to recollect ideal forms gathered from the immortal soul
C: Forms must exist before our birth that we collect as the immortal soul
```
