### Major Arcana (Revised)
- [[The Fool]]: Remove this
- [[The Magician]]: Held card. damage to one enemy deals 2 damage to another enemy
- [[The High Priestess]]: Bring bottom most card to top most card in draw pile
- [[The Empress]]: Held, acts as a second consumable slot
- [[The Emperor]]: Held, consumable, Reset damage on weapon
- [[The Hierophant]]: Held, take 4 instance of damage
- [[The Lovers]]: Heal two
- [[The Chariot]]: Held, consumable, search draw pile for a weapon or health, then shuffle drawed cards and move them to the bottom
- [[Magic/Justice|Justice]]: Held, +2 damage when health under 10
- [[The Hermit]]: Take one of four cards from discard
- [[The Wheel of Fortune]]: Shuffle the draw deck
- [[Strength]]: Held card. You deal +3 barehand damage
- [[The Hanged Man]]: Pentacles can be used as cups, cups can be used as pentacles
- [[Death]]: Search discard for 1 card, bring it back to life and bring it to hand or board
- [[Temperance]]: Consume a weapon/potion, destroy an enemy
- [[The Tower]]: Destroy topmost 2 cards in draw pile
- [[The Devil]]: You cant heal, deal +2 damage
- [[The Star]]: Look at discard for a specific card and move it to bottom of draw pile
- [[The Moon]]: See next 6 cards
- [[The Sun]]: Heal all health
- [[Philosophy/Judgement|Judgement]]: Lose 5 health, wipe out board
- [[The World]]: Held. Weapon no longer takes damage for one instance
